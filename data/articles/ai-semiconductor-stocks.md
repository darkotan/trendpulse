# Top AI Semiconductor Stocks to Watch in 2026 — Beyond NVIDIA

NVIDIA dominates the AI chip conversation, but the AI semiconductor ecosystem is far bigger than one company. Here are the key players and sub-sectors investors should watch.

## The GPU Duopoly: NVIDIA vs AMD

**NVIDIA (NVDA)** — The undisputed leader with 80%+ GPU market share. H100 and B200 chips are the gold standard for AI training. Revenue exploded from $27B to $130B+ in two years.

**AMD (AMD)** — The only credible challenger. MI300 series is gaining traction with cloud customers who want a second source. Lisa Su has positioned AMD as the "value alternative" to NVIDIA.

## The CPO/Photonics Revolution

Co-Packaged Optics (CPO) is the next frontier. As AI clusters get bigger, the bottleneck shifts from compute to connectivity. Companies in this space include:

- **Marvell (MRVL)** — Custom ASIC + optical interconnect
- **Lumentum (LITE)** — Optical components and 3D sensing
- **Applied Optoelectronics (AAOI)** — 800G/1.6T optical modules

## The Memory/Storage Layer

AI needs massive amounts of fast storage:
- **SanDisk (SNDK)** — NAND flash for data centers
- **Micron (MU)** — HBM memory, critical for GPU performance

## The Full Supply Chain

From chip equipment (ASML, AMAT) to packaging (Amkor) to networking (Arista), the AI hardware ecosystem creates opportunities beyond just chip designers.
